Summary
===============================

Summary of progress in February 2025

- I learned that gradient descent (first order in time) works slightly better than leapfrog (second order in time). This observation was made only on stationary videos, so it's not conclusive.
- I finally cracked the BALLS dataset. iP-VAE is the only model that fully untangles that dataset, with almost perfect r2 on both reconstruction, as well as latent regression.
    - Therefore: iP-VAE does learn a compositional code.
- I ran many experiments for both Gaussian and Gaussian + Activation iterative models.
    - None of them compete with iP-VAE.
- Gaussian iterative models don't really work on whitened images, so I ran them on non-whitened images:
    - Gaus+ReLU: learns some center-surround looking units, but is unstable.
    - Gaus+Sigmoid: learns the best center-surrounds. Both ON and OFF cells.
    - The results are dataset dependent: Kyoto results in cleaner center-surrounds, but I could get it working on van Hateren as well (but less clean).
- Next up: compiling results, writing up the paper. The deadline for putting on arxiv is March 15.
